Privacy Policy

Hidden Reasons Studio · last updated September 29, 2026

What this app is

Hidden Reasons Studio is a private tool used only by the owner of the Hidden Reasons YouTube channel. It uploads and schedules the owner's own approved videos to the owner's own channel. It has no other users.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What it accesses and why

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's approved video and schedule it.
  • Manage captions (youtube.force-ssl): to add the video's caption file and set its cover image, and to check that the upload is scheduled correctly.

It never deletes videos, changes other videos, reads or posts comments, or touches any other part of your Google Account.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google gives the app is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private file only the owner's account can read. The app keeps a local record of which videos it posted and when. Nothing from your Google Account is sent anywhere except back to Google.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with, or shown to anyone else.

How to remove access

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account's security settings: myaccount.google.com/connections. Deleting the token file on the computer also stops the app.

Deleting data

Removing access (above) makes Google cancel the app's sign-in. The owner can also delete the sign-in file and the app's local posting record from the computer at any time. The app keeps no other copy of your Google data.
